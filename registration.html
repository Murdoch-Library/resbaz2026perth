Who can register?

✔️ Researchers at ALL levels and from ALL disciplines
✔️ PhD candidates, research masters students, and early career researchers
✔️ Research technologists and software engineers supporting research
✔️ All university or government-funded staff or students welcome!

Please note: commercial attendees are only permitted to attend through sponsorship agreements. Please get in touch for more details.

Accessibility Grants Available

To support inclusive participation in ResBaz Perth 2026, we are offering a limited number of Accessibility Grants to assist students and researchers who may face barriers to attending. These grants are intended to support participants from underrepresented backgrounds, those with caring responsibilities, individuals with accessibility needs, or those experiencing financial constraints. We are aiming to offer up to ten grants, each covering the full cost of registration for all three days of the event.
